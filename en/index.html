Our Process

Rigorous QC, a complete service workflow

We insist on a five-step human translation workflow, with multi-layer quality assurance across pre-sale, in-progress and after-sale.

1

Pre-sale Analysis

Understand requirements in detail, analyze the project fully and provide a professional solution — the first step of quality control.

2

Confirm & Assign

Confirm the project, process payment, and match the best specialist linguists by industry and language.

3

Translation

Industry-specialist translators do the work, focusing on consistent terminology and localized expression.

4

Proofread & Review

Strictly follow the five-step flow — translate, proofread, review, typeset, confirm — at every layer.

5

Typesetting & Confirm

Professional typesetting restores layout, final confirmation and delivery; questions answered anytime.

6

After-sale Warranty

Free translation storage for 3 months, one free revision within the storage period, long-term free consulting.
